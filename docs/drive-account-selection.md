# Google Drive account selection

When more than one Google Drive account is connected, every storage action must make the target account explicit.

- Account-level actions identify the target Drive account by email.
- Preparing an account provisions unassigned active shows into that account and repairs workspaces already assigned to that same account.
- Shows already assigned to a different Drive account are not moved by the account-level prepare action.
- Per-show actions continue to show the specific target Drive account.
- Google Drive files are never deleted merely because a show is reassigned or deleted from Studio metadata.
